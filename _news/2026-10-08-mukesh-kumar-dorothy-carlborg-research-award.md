Congratulations to **Dr Mukesh Kumar** on receiving a **2026 Dorothy Carlborg Research Award** from the **Cornea and Contact Lens Society of Australia (CCLSA)**!

The Dorothy Carlborg Research Award supports research in the cornea and contact lens field. This is the **second time** Mukesh has received the award, following his previous success in 2023.

Mukesh's research focuses on the ocular surface, tear film and contact lens wear, with particular interests in contact lens discomfort, scleral lenses, corneal physiology and ocular surface health.

This recognition comes in a remarkable year for Mukesh, who was also awarded a 2026 Ezell Fellowship by the American Academy of Optometry Foundation. The whole lab is proud to celebrate his continued success. Well done, Mukesh!
