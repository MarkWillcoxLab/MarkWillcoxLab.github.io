June 2026 was a big month for microbiology in the Willcox Lab. Colleagues from the **School of Optometry and Vision Science (SOVS), UNSW Sydney** represented the group at two flagship ASM meetings on opposite sides of the Pacific: **[ASM Microbe 2026](https://asm.org/events/asm-microbe)** in Washington, D.C. and the **[ASM National Meeting 2026](https://www.theasmmeeting.org.au/)** in Melbourne.
