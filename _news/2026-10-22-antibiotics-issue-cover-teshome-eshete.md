Our research has been selected as the **Issue Cover of *Antibiotics*, Volume 15, Issue 8 (August 2026)**!

The featured article, **"Targeting WHO-Priority MDR and XDR Gram-Negative Ocular Isolates by Combining Conventional Antimicrobials with Membrane-Active Peptides"**, explores strategies to combat the drug-resistant pathogens responsible for severe eye infections. The cover, *"Peptide–Antibiotic Combinations Restore Activity Against MDR Ocular Pathogens"*, highlights how combining antibiotics with the antimicrobial peptide Mel4 or colistin increases membrane permeability, achieving up to 4096-fold reductions in MIC and enhanced biofilm reduction.

**Authors:** Teshome Belachew Eshete, Shyam Kumar Mishra, Naresh Kumar and Mark Willcox

Congratulations to **Teshome** and the team on this fantastic recognition of their work!
