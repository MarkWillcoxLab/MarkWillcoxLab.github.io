**Professor Mark Willcox** attended the **27th International Society for Eye Research (ISER) meeting** in Valencia, Spain, one of the premier international meetings in eye research.

**Venue:** Valencia, Spain  

**Dates:** 23rd to 27th August 2026

## Talks from our School

The UNSW School of Optometry and Vision Science was very well represented this year, with **A/Prof Nicole Carnt**, **Dr Jerome Ozkan**, **A/Prof Michele Madigan** and **Dr Daisy Shu** also attending.

- **Prof Mark Willcox** gave a talk entitled *"The Neonatal Ocular Surface Microbiome"*, presenting research by Jerome and Mark in collaboration with the Manipal Academy of Higher Education.
- **Dr Jerome Ozkan** presented *"Comparative Analysis of the Ocular Microbiome in MGD and Healthy Eyes across Multiple Ocular Sites"*.
- **A/Prof Nicole Carnt** presented *"Clinical and Microbiome Profiles of Acanthamoeba Keratitis in Sydney, Australia"*.

Jerome and Nicole also each chaired a session at the meeting.

**Reflection:** "Great conference." It was a proud moment to see our School's ocular microbiome and infection research shared on the international stage, and we congratulate everyone who presented and chaired sessions.
