Members of the Willcox Lab represented the **School of Optometry and Vision Science (SOVS), UNSW Sydney** at the **[ASM National Meeting 2026](https://www.theasmmeeting.org.au/)**, the Australian Society for Microbiology’s premier annual conference on microbiology.

<p class="mb-0"><a href="{{ '/news/asm-2026/' | relative_url }}">← Back to ASM 2026 overview (Melbourne &amp; Washington, D.C.)</a></p>

**Venue:** Melbourne Convention and Exhibition Centre, Melbourne VIC  

**Dates:** 15–18 June 2026

The meeting brought together scientists, clinical researchers, and industry professionals to share the latest advances in microbiology. Lab members presented posters on ocular microbiology and *Staphylococcus aureus* genomics, and colleagues including Dr. Umme Laila Urmi, Suleman Irfan, and Biruk Bayleyegn Belete also joined the Willcox Lab delegation.
