We are delighted to celebrate **Professor Mark Willcox**, who has received the **Cornea & Contact Lens Society of Australia (CCLSA) Brien Holden Memorial Award**, jointly with **Professor Eric Papas**.

The award was presented at the **20th International Cornea and Contact Lens Congress (ICCLC 2026)**, themed *"The Art of Possibility"*, where Mark and Eric delivered the **Brien Holden Memorial Lecture**, *"The Things That Oxygen Can't Fix"*.

**Venue:** Crowne Plaza, Hobart, Tasmania  

**Dates:** 16th to 18th October 2026

The Brien Holden Memorial Award honours the legacy of Professor Brien Holden, whose pioneering work transformed contact lens research and eye care worldwide. Joining past recipients who have shaped the field, this recognition reflects Mark's outstanding contributions to cornea and contact lens science, particularly in ocular microbiology, contact lens-related infection and antimicrobial strategies.

It was a special congress for the School: **Scientia Professor Fiona Stapleton** was also honoured with the CCLSA **Kenneth W. Bell Medal**.

Congratulations, Mark, on this well-deserved honour!
