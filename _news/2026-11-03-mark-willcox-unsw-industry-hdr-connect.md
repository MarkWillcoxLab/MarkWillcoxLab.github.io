**Professor Mark Willcox** from the School of Optometry and Vision Science at UNSW Sydney participated as an invited panellist at the **Term 3 UNSW Industry HDR Connect**, held on 3 November 2026 at the Michael Crouch Innovation Centre.

**Venue:** Michael Crouch Innovation Centre, UNSW Sydney  

**Date:** 3rd November 2026

The event brought together HDR candidates, academic researchers, and industry representatives for an engaging panel discussion exploring how academia and industry can collaborate to address real-world challenges, drive innovation, and create meaningful research opportunities.

Drawing on his extensive experience working with industry partners, Professor Willcox shared insights into developing successful academic-industry collaborations and translating research discoveries into practical applications.

The panel also featured **Andrew Kobylinski**, CEO of Lumicare, an Australian medical technology company, and **Daniel Massaioli**, Founder and Director of SAN-AIR Australia Pty Ltd, an Australian manufacturer. Both are close industry collaborators of Professor Willcox and shared valuable perspectives on academic-industry partnerships from an industry standpoint. **Lucien Alperstein**, a UNSW PhD candidate collaborating with industry partner Sea Forest, also contributed insights from the perspective of industry-integrated doctoral research.

The event concluded with a networking session, providing opportunities for HDR candidates to connect with researchers and industry professionals and explore potential research collaborations and career pathways beyond academia.
