One of our papers published in ***Scientific Reports*** has been featured in **Sciworthy**, in the article *"Scientists found a compound that can disarm a drug-resistant bacterium"* by Halimat Chisom Atanda.

The article highlights our study, **"Multimodal computational discovery of MvfR inhibitors targeting quorum sensing in multi-drug-resistant *Pseudomonas aeruginosa*"**, which used genome data from recent clinical isolates, AlphaFold2 protein modelling and drug-likeness screening to identify compounds that could disarm *P. aeruginosa* by blocking MvfR, a core protein in its quorum-sensing system.

**Authors:** Tope Abraham Ibisanmi, Xiaotao Jiang, Rasel Ahmed Khan, Tsz Tin (Tom) Yu, Mark Willcox and Naresh Kumar

It is exciting to see our research reaching a wider audience. Congratulations to **Tope** and all the authors, and special thanks to Professor Mark Willcox and Professor Naresh Kumar for making this research possible.
