Congratulations to **Elias Shiferaw Mekonen**, who has been awarded the **Scientia PhD Global Mobility Grant** by the **UNSW Graduate Research School**!

This competitive award will enable Elias to establish international research collaborations and advance PhD research on **antimicrobial peptides and peptidomimetics as new therapies against antimicrobial-resistant infections**, an area of urgent global need as resistance to last-resort antibiotics continues to rise.

The grant caps an outstanding year for Elias. Elias's work on improving the safety profiles of new antimicrobials reflects exactly the kind of rigorous, translational science that this grant is designed to support.

> "I am sincerely grateful to my supervisors for their guidance and encouragement, and in particular to Professor Mark Willcox, whose mentorship, expertise and continued support have been instrumental throughout my PhD. My thanks also to the Graduate Research School for this opportunity, and to our collaborators. Excited for the discoveries and collaborations ahead!"

Dedication, curiosity and drive make Elias a valued member of the Willcox Lab, and this recognition is thoroughly deserved. The whole lab is proud of Elias and looks forward to the international partnerships and discoveries this grant will make possible. Well done, Elias!
