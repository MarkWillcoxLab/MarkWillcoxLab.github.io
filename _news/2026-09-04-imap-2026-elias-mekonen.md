Congratulations to **Elias Shiferaw Mekonen**, who presented PhD research at the **13th International Meeting on Antimicrobial Peptides (IMAP 2026)**, delivering both a **rapid oral talk** and a **poster** to an international audience of leading antimicrobial peptide researchers.

**Conference:** 13th International Meeting on Antimicrobial Peptides (IMAP 2026)  

**Venue:** Faculty of Science, University of Split, Croatia  

**Dates:** 2nd to 4th September 2026

**Abstract title**

Therapeutic potential and safety challenges of antimicrobial peptides and peptidomimetics against ESKAPEE pathogens: a systematic quantitative analysis

**Brief description of poster/talk**

The study is a systematic review with quantitative analysis of antimicrobial peptides (AMPs) and peptidomimetics tested against the ESKAPEE pathogens, the priority group of bacteria behind many drug-resistant hospital infections. It brings together published data on antimicrobial potency alongside safety measures such as cytotoxicity, haemolysis and nephrotoxicity, and uses therapeutic window and selectivity index analysis to show where these compounds stand between efficacy and toxicity. The work highlights the translational wall that still limits clinical use of AMPs, and points to chemical modification and nanocarrier delivery as promising strategies to widen their therapeutic window.

**Reflection on conference experience**

"Presenting at IMAP 2026 was a highlight of my PhD journey. Sharing my work with an international audience of leading AMP researchers generated valuable discussion after my talk and at the poster session, and the feedback has given me fresh perspectives for the next stages of my project. I also had the chance to meet and connect with researchers working on peptide design and safety, leading to inspiring conversations with real potential for future collaboration.

This work would not have been possible without the guidance and support of all my supervisors, particularly Prof. Mark Willcox. I also thank UNSW for DRTG and the IMAP 2026 organisers for hosting such a well-run and inspiring meeting in Split."
