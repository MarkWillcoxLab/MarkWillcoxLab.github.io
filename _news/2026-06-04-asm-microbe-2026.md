Members of the Willcox Lab represented the **School of Optometry and Vision Science (SOVS), UNSW Sydney** at **[ASM Microbe 2026](https://asm.org/events/asm-microbe)**, the American Society for Microbiology’s flagship international meeting on the microbial sciences.

<p class="mb-0"><a href="{{ '/news/asm-2026/' | relative_url }}">← Back to ASM 2026 overview (Melbourne &amp; Washington, D.C.)</a></p>

**Venue:** Walter E. Washington Convention Center, Washington, D.C., USA  

**Dates:** 4–7 June 2026

The meeting brought together researchers, clinicians, and industry leaders to share advances in microbiology, antimicrobial resistance, and therapeutic innovation. Members of the Willcox Lab presented posters and represented SOVS, UNSW Sydney at the conference.
