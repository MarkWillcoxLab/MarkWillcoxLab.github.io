Recently, Mark Willcox visited [A*STAR Infectious Diseases Labs](https://www.a-star.edu.sg/idlabs), where he delivered an engaging seminar on antimicrobial peptides (AMPs) and the broader challenge of antimicrobial resistance (AMR). His presentation provided valuable insights into current research and emerging strategies to combat infectious diseases.

During the visit, discussions highlighted the strong alignment and complementary nature of research interests between Prof. Willcox and the A*IDL team, creating exciting opportunities for future collaboration in addressing AMR and advancing infectious disease research.
