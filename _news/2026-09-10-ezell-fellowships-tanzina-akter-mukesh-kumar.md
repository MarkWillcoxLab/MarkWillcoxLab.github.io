We are thrilled to congratulate **Tanzina Akter** and **Mukesh Kumar** from our lab, who have been awarded prestigious **2026 Ezell Fellowships** by the **American Academy of Optometry Foundation**.

They are among four UNSW School of Optometry & Vision Science researchers recognised this year, and among just **16 researchers selected internationally** for this highly competitive program. Since 1949, Ezell Fellowships have supported graduate students in full-time vision research programs. The 2026 fellowship includes a prize award and two travel grants to attend the annual meetings of the American Academy of Optometry and the Association for Research in Vision and Ophthalmology.

## Tanzina Akter — AAO Centennial Ezell Fellowship

**Research project:** Resistance characteristics and virulence factors of *Pseudomonas aeruginosa* from microbial keratitis

> "Receiving the 2026 AAO Centennial Ezell Fellowship from the American Academy of Optometry Foundation is an incredible honour and a meaningful milestone in my journey as an emerging vision scientist. I am especially grateful to have my research on sight-threatening ocular infection associated with contact lenses and antimicrobial resistance recognised by such a prestigious organisation."

## Mukesh Kumar — AAO Section on Cornea, Contact Lenses, and Refractive Technologies (CCLRT) Ezell Fellow

**Research project:** Examination of physiological changes that affect comfort during daily disposable silicone hydrogel contact lens wear

This is the **second time** Mukesh has received an Ezell Fellowship, a remarkable achievement given how competitive these awards are.

> "I am deeply honoured to receive an Ezell Fellowship for the second time. I am sincerely grateful to the American Academy of Optometry Foundation for this recognition and continued support. My heartfelt thanks to my supervisor, Professor Mark Willcox, for his invaluable mentorship, encouragement, and guidance throughout my research journey."

Congratulations, Tanzina and Mukesh! We are incredibly proud of you both, and we also congratulate fellow UNSW recipients Dereje Anbesse and Soumen Sadhu.
